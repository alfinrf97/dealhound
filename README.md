DealHound — a Black Friday & Cyber Monday 2026 deal finder. 45 real deals across 7 stores (Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, Target, Lenovo, Google Store, Newegg), every price checked on Black Friday, 27 Nov 2026, each with its store link, sale price, usual price, discount, check time, source, and an honest verdict (new low, strong price, or routine sale) with evidence. Search and filters by category, store and discount, sorting by discount, saving, price or new lows, and a phone layout.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
